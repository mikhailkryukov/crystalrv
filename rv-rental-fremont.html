FAQ

Common Questions

How far is Crystal RV from Fremont?
We're about 18 miles from Fremont — roughly a twenty-five-minute drive across the Dumbarton Bridge (Hwy 84) or up Interstate 880 to our Mountain View base at 1001 La Avenida St.
Do you deliver across the bay to Fremont?
Yes. We deliver throughout the East Bay Tri-City area — Fremont, Newark, Union City, and beyond. Rentals of 5+ nights receive complimentary delivery within our primary service zone.
Can I self-pickup the RV?
Yes — self-pickup is always free at our Mountain View base, daily 8AM–6PM by appointment, with a full 30–60 minute orientation. Fremont guests usually arrive in about twenty-five minutes.
Is Fremont closer to Yosemite?
A little. From the East Bay, Interstate 580 connects to Highway 120 toward Yosemite, so Fremont travelers often save time versus crossing back through the South Bay.
What's the minimum rental period?
The minimum rental is 3 nights for all vehicles, with no maximum. Many Fremont guests take longer 1–3 week California road trips.
