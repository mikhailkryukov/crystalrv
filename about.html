Who We Are

Adventurers, Just Like You

Crystal RV was founded by Michael and Xeniya, a Bay Area family who kept running into the same problem: renting an RV was needlessly stressful. Peer-to-peer listings were inconsistent, corporate fleets were overpriced and impersonal, and first-timers were left to figure everything out on their own.

So we built something different. We specialize in hosting first-time RV travelers with turn-key, all-inclusive motorhomes: fully stocked, professionally inspected, and handed over with a personal orientation, either at our Mountain View base or delivered to your door.

Every RV in our fleet has carried us, our kids and our dog across California, from Yosemite and Big Sur to Lake Tahoe and the Redwood Coast. When we give you campground advice, it's from nights we've actually spent there. That's also why we write our trip guides.
