FAQ

Common Questions

How far is Lake Tahoe from Crystal RV?
Lake Tahoe is about 210 miles — roughly four hours — from our Mountain View base, via I-580/I-80 to the North and West Shores or US-50 to South Lake Tahoe.
Which Lake Tahoe campgrounds take RVs?
Around the lake, D.L. Bliss and Emerald Bay State Parks, Fallen Leaf, Tahoe Valley, Zephyr Cove, and Campground by the Lake accept RVs within posted length limits. Summer sites book up months in advance.
Do I need campground reservations?
In summer, yes — Tahoe's popular campgrounds fill months ahead. Reserve early through Recreation.gov, ReserveCalifornia, or the campground operator. We can point you to the right site for each location.
Can the RV handle the mountain drive and winter conditions?
Yes, our Class C motorhomes are comfortable on I-80 and US-50. In winter, chain controls are common in the Sierra — we'll review current conditions and any seasonal notes during your orientation.
What should I know about altitude and bears at Tahoe?
Tahoe sits above 6,000 feet, so take it easy the first day and stay hydrated. It's also bear country — store food in provided lockers and never leave it out. We include tips and the RV has secure interior storage.
