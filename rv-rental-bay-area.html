FAQ

Bay Area RV Rental Questions

What makes Crystal RV the best RV rental in the Bay Area?
Three things set us apart: (1) personal door-to-door delivery with expert orientation — we don't make you come to us; (2) turn-key ready vehicles with 100+ items already stocked inside; (3) genuine 24/7 human support during your trip. We're a family-run business that treats every rental like our own trip, which is why we maintain a perfect 5.0-star Google rating.
What RV types do you rent in the Bay Area?
We specialize in Class C motorhomes — the sweet spot between drivability and livability. All four vehicles in our fleet are 24–25ft, sleep 6, and come fully equipped. They're easy enough for first-timers to drive but spacious enough for a full family vacation. We don't offer Class A coaches or travel trailers — we focus on doing Class C exceptionally well.
How much does RV rental in the Bay Area cost?
Nightly rates vary by vehicle and season. All rentals include 100 miles/day, expert orientation, full equipment, $1M insurance, and 24/7 support. A $1,500 refundable deposit and one-time $115 cleaning/prep fee apply. Contact us or use the booking widget above for exact pricing on your dates. Rentals of 5+ nights receive complimentary delivery anywhere in our primary Bay Area zone.
Do you deliver to the East Bay, Marin, and Peninsula?
Yes — we serve all Bay Area sub-regions: the Peninsula (San Mateo, Redwood City, Palo Alto), East Bay (Oakland, Berkeley, Fremont, Walnut Creek), Marin County (San Rafael, Novato, Tiburon), North Bay (Napa, Sonoma), and South Bay (San Jose, Santa Clara, Sunnyvale). Delivery fees and complimentary delivery windows vary by distance.
What is the cancellation policy for Bay Area RV rentals?
Full refund for cancellations 5+ days before pickup. 75% refund for cancellations under 5 days. Add-ons are always fully refundable. We also offer a Trip Protection Plan (6% of total) that allows penalty-free rescheduling up to 4 days before your departure. Travel credits never expire.
