FAQ

San Jose RV Rental Questions

Do you deliver RVs to all parts of San Jose?
Yes — we deliver anywhere in San Jose including Willow Glen, Almaden, Evergreen, Berryessa, Blossom Hill, and the downtown area. Rentals of 5+ nights receive complimentary delivery within our primary service zone. Shorter rentals have a delivery fee based on your distance from Mountain View. We also deliver to SJC airport.
How far is Crystal RV from San Jose?
Our base is in Mountain View — about 12 miles north of downtown San Jose via Hwy 85. We typically deliver within 30–45 minutes. Prefer to skip the delivery fee? You're welcome to self-pickup from our Mountain View location (1001 La Avenida St) with no charge.
Can I pick up the RV myself in Mountain View?
Absolutely. Self-pickup is always available at our Mountain View address for no delivery charge. We run pickups daily 8AM–6PM by appointment and still include the full orientation walkthrough.
What's the best RV trip from San Jose?
Our most popular trip from San Jose is Yosemite (3.5 hrs). Pinnacles National Park is a hidden gem just 1.5 hours south — fantastic for spring wildflowers. Big Sur is a stunning 2-hour coastal drive. For cooler weather, Lake Tahoe is about 4 hours northeast and incredible in summer and fall.
Do I need a special license to drive the RV in California?
No special license needed. A standard California driver's license (or valid out-of-state/international license) is all that's required. Drivers must be 25+ with 2+ years of licensed driving experience. We'll walk you through everything at delivery — first-timers feel confident immediately.
How much does it cost to rent an RV in San Jose?
Nightly rates vary by vehicle and season. All rentals include 100 miles/day, door-to-door delivery (5+ nights), expert orientation, full equipment, $1M insurance, and 24/7 support. A $1,500 refundable deposit applies. Contact us or use the booking widget above for exact pricing on your dates.
