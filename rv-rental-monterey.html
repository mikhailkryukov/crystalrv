FAQ

Common Questions

How far is Monterey from Crystal RV?
Monterey is about 75 miles — roughly 90 minutes — from our Mountain View base, via US-101 and CA-156 to Highway 1.
Which Monterey-area campgrounds take RVs?
Veterans Memorial Park in Monterey, Laguna Seca Recreation Area, Marina Dunes RV Park, and Saddle Mountain near Carmel accept RVs. Coastal sites book up in summer, so reserve ahead.
Do I need campground reservations?
In summer and on weekends, yes. Monterey Peninsula campgrounds are popular — reserve through the park or operator in advance. We can point you to the right site for each campground.
Can I drive the RV on 17-Mile Drive?
17-Mile Drive permits passenger vehicles and RVs, but it's scenic and slow with size considerations at the gates. Many guests prefer to park and explore Pebble Beach and the coast at a relaxed pace — check current vehicle rules before you go.
Is this a good trip for families?
Very. The Monterey Bay Aquarium, easy beaches, and Carmel village are family favorites, and our motorhomes sleep up to six with everything included.
