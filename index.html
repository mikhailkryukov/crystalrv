Help & Policies

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about renting from Crystal RV — policies, pricing, pets, and more.

👤 Eligibility & Drivers
Who can rent a Crystal RV? ▾
All renters must be 25 years of age or older with a valid driver's license held for at least two years. A clean driving record is required — no DUIs or reckless driving in the past 5 years. We welcome domestic and international guests; our insurance partners cover international drivers with a valid license.
Can there be additional drivers? ▾
Yes — we encourage you to list all adults over 25 who will drive at the time of booking so they're fully covered by insurance. There's no additional charge for listing extra drivers. All drivers must meet the same eligibility requirements.
Do I need a special license to drive an RV? ▾
No special license is required. Our vehicles are designed to be driven by anyone with a standard driver's license. They handle much easier than you'd expect — and we include a full orientation so you'll feel confident before you pull out of the driveway.
Do you work with out-of-state and international guests? ▾
Absolutely! Crystal RV is perfect for anyone visiting the Bay Area from out of state or internationally. Our insurance covers international drivers, and our fleet is fully equipped for any traveler. We also offer SFO airport delivery for arriving guests — just let us know your flight details.
📅 Booking & Reservations
What is the minimum rental period? ▾
The minimum rental is 3 nights for most RVs. Certain holidays (Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's) require a 7-night minimum. There's no maximum — many guests take extended 2–3 week California road trips with us.
How far in advance should I book? ▾
For peak season (May–September) and holiday weekends, we recommend booking 6–10 weeks ahead. Our Class C motorhomes fill up fastest. Off-season availability is more flexible, but popular RVs can still book up — don't wait too long!
Is a deposit required? ▾
Yes, a refundable security deposit of $1,500 is required for all rentals. It's fully refunded within 5 business days of return, provided the RV comes back in the same condition. This deposit applies to all vehicles including pet-friendly rentals.
What payment methods do you accept? ▾
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover). We also offer financing through Affirm — pay in 3, 6, or 12 monthly installments. Split the cost of your trip into easy payments and book today.
Can I modify or extend my reservation? ▾
Yes! Contact us as soon as possible to check availability. If the RV is available, we're happy to extend. If you need just a few extra hours, extensions are $20/hour (up to 8 hours, latest return by 8PM). Full day extensions are charged at the standard nightly rate. Additional balance must be paid before the RV's return.
Do you offer one-way trips? ▾
We do offer one-way trips on a case-by-case basis. If you'd like to pick up in the Bay Area and drop off elsewhere in California, or vice versa, contact us and we'll create a custom quote for you.
🔄 Cancellation Policy
What is your cancellation policy? ▾
Full refund if cancelled 5 or more days before your pickup date. 75% refund if cancelled fewer than 5 days before pickup. Add-ons (bikes, paddle boards, accessories) are always fully refundable. We also offer a Trip Protection add-on (6% of reservation) that lets you reschedule up to 4 days before departure with no penalty — must be purchased within 24 hours of booking.
Do travel credits expire? ▾
No — travel credits issued by Crystal RV never expire. Use them anytime for any future reservation. We want you to take that trip, even if life got in the way this time.
What if there's a weather emergency or natural disaster? ▾
In the event of a declared state of emergency, wildfire, flood, or severe weather making travel unsafe, we offer full rebooking credits regardless of notice. Your safety is our priority — always. Please call us so we can help you reschedule.
🚐 The RV & What's Included
What's included with every Crystal RV rental? ▾
Every rental arrives fully stocked: complete cookware set (pots, pans, dishes, silverware, utensils, cutting boards, can opener, cheese grater), kitchen supplies (garbage bags, dish soap, paper towels, broom), RV toilet paper, first aid kit, appliances (refrigerator, microwave, 2-burner gas stove, K-cup coffee maker, electric kettle, toaster), maintenance kit (sewer hoses, gloves, electrical adapters, leveling blocks), fresh bed sheets, TV + HDMI cord, stereo, board games, up to 6 camp chairs at no charge. Groceries and towel/blanket sets are not included (linens add-on: $15/set).
Are pets allowed? ▾
We love your furry companions! Pets are welcome in designated pet-friendly RVs (look for 🐾 in the listing). A $100 pet fee applies per trip, and the security deposit increases to $1,500. Pets must be potty trained and may not be left unattended in the RV. The vehicle must be returned free of pet hair and odor. Dogs, cats, and birds are all welcome.
Is mileage included? ▾
Each rental includes 100 free miles per day. Additional miles are billed at $0.45/mile — there are no refunds on unused miles. We recommend estimating your route mileage before booking, especially for longer road trips to destinations like Yosemite, Tahoe, or Big Sur. Promotional reservations may have different mileage terms.
Is there a cleaning fee? ▾
Yes, a one-time preparation and cleaning fee of $115 applies to all reservations. This covers deep cleaning, sanitization, and restocking the RV to its full inventory before your trip.
Is smoking allowed in the RV? ▾
Smoking (tobacco or marijuana) is strictly prohibited inside all Crystal RV vehicles. If the RV is returned with any smoke odor, a $700 deep-cleaning fee will be charged. Please enjoy California's fresh air outside!
Can I take the RV to the snow (Lake Tahoe, Sierras)? ▾
Yes! All of our RVs are snow-ready and we include chains at no charge (by request). For temperatures below 40°F overnight, you'll want to book campgrounds with electrical hookups to run the furnace. Our team can advise on winter campground options when you book.
Are there places the RV cannot go? ▾
Our RVs are not permitted off-road or into Mexico. Some high-clearance wilderness roads may be restricted depending on vehicle size — we'll flag any concerns based on your itinerary during the orientation.
Can I tow my car or boat? ▾
Yes — our Class C motorhomes can tow up to 5,000 lbs. Please let us know your towing plans at booking so we can set up the appropriate hitch and walk you through towing procedures during your orientation.
What do I need to do before returning the RV? ▾
Before return, please: refill the fuel tank with diesel to the level you received it, dump the gray and black water tanks (we offer this as a $45 service if you prefer), and return the RV in a reasonably clean condition. We'll give you a full checklist. If you can't refuel, we charge a $10 service fee plus the cost of fuel.
🛡️ Insurance & Accidents
What insurance is included? ▾
Every Crystal RV rental includes $1 million in liability coverage and comprehensive collision protection up to the vehicle's value. You're also automatically covered for interior damage up to $500 at no charge. You do NOT need to add RV coverage to your personal auto insurance policy. Additional protection upgrades are available at booking.
What happens if I'm in an accident? ▾
Call us right away so we can assist you through the process. Take detailed photos of all vehicles and property involved, and photograph the other party's insurance information and driver's license. Do not admit fault. Your insurance will cover the incident less the applicable deductible. We're here to guide you through every step.
What if I accidentally damage something inside? ▾
Accidents happen — we get it. Let us know as soon as possible so we can order replacement parts or schedule repairs. Interior damages up to $500 are automatically covered at no charge. For damages beyond $500, your security deposit may be applied. Honesty always leads to the best outcome; please don't try to hide damage.
What happens if the RV breaks down on the road? ▾
Call us immediately at (669) 245-5636 — we answer 24/7. We'll coordinate roadside assistance, which is included in every rental. Mechanical failures are fully covered. If the vehicle cannot be repaired in a timely manner, we'll arrange alternative transportation or accommodations. We've never left a guest stranded.
What if I get a ticket or parking citation? ▾
The renter is fully responsible for all traffic tickets, parking citations, and any associated fees incurred during the rental period.
