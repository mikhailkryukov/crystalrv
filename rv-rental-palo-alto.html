FAQ

Common Questions

Where exactly is Crystal RV located?
We're at 1001 La Avenida St, Mountain View, CA 94043 — just off Hwy 101, between the Rengstorff Ave and Shoreline Blvd exits. It's a 10-minute drive from downtown Palo Alto via El Camino Real.
Can I self-pickup the RV in Mountain View?
Yes — self-pickup is always free. We run pickups daily 8AM–6PM by appointment and include the full 30–60 minute orientation. Palo Alto guests typically arrive in under 10 minutes.
Do you deliver to Palo Alto, Menlo Park, and Redwood City?
Yes. We deliver throughout the Peninsula — Palo Alto, Menlo Park, Los Altos, Redwood City, San Mateo, and beyond. Rentals of 5+ nights receive complimentary delivery within our primary service zone.
What's the minimum rental period?
The minimum rental is 3 nights for all vehicles. There's no maximum — many guests take extended 1–3 week California road trips.
What campgrounds are near Palo Alto?
Closest options: Portola Redwoods State Park (45 min), Henry Cowell Redwoods (50 min), Butano State Park (50 min). For a weekend trip, Big Basin Redwoods (1 hr) is spectacular. Yosemite Valley (3.5 hrs) is our most popular full-trip destination for Palo Alto guests.
