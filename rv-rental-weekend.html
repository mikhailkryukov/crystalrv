FAQ

Common Questions

What is the minimum rental period?
Our minimum rental is 3 nights for all vehicles. This is ideal for a long weekend (Friday to Monday) or a mid-week escape. There's no maximum — many guests take 1–3 week trips.
What's the best weekend RV trip from the Bay Area?
Our most popular weekend destinations: Bodega Bay (2 hrs north — stunning ocean camping), Pinnacles National Park (2 hrs south — caves and condors), Big Sur (2.5 hrs south — iconic cliffside views), and Point Reyes (1.5 hrs — wild beaches and lighthouse). All are reachable the same day as delivery.
Can I book a weekend RV rental last minute?
Yes — if availability exists, we can often accommodate bookings with 24–48 hours notice. For peak weekends (Memorial Day, 4th of July, Labor Day), we recommend booking 4–6 weeks in advance. Check live availability in our booking widget above.
What time do you deliver on Friday?
We offer delivery windows from 8AM–8PM. Most weekend guests choose an 8–10AM Friday delivery so they can be at their campsite by early afternoon. We confirm exact timing 24 hours before.
Are there any blackout dates or holiday minimums?
Major holidays (Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's, Memorial Day weekend) may require a 7-night minimum. Contact us if you're booking around a major holiday to confirm availability and minimum stay requirements.
