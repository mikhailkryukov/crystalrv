Where is Crystal RV located in Mountain View?
We're at 1001 La Avenida St, Mountain View, CA 94043, just off Hwy 101 between the Rengstorff Ave and Shoreline Blvd exits — a short drive from Castro Street, the Googleplex, and NASA Ames.
Is self-pickup free for Mountain View locals?
Yes — self-pickup is always free. We run pickups daily from 8AM–6PM by appointment and include a full 30–60 minute orientation. Most Mountain View guests are done in under half an hour.
Can you deliver the RV to my Mountain View home?
Absolutely. We deliver to any Mountain View address — home, office, or trailhead — and complete the orientation right there. Rentals of 5+ nights receive complimentary local delivery.

The minimum is 3 nights for all vehicles, with no maximum. Many local guests take extended 1–3 week California road trips.

For an easy overnight, Half Moon Bay and Santa Cruz are under an hour. For a full trip, Yosemite Valley (3.5 hrs) is our most popular destination, with Big Sur and Lake Tahoe close behind.
