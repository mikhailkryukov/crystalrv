FAQ

Common Questions

How far is Crystal RV from SFO?
Our Mountain View base at 1001 La Avenida St is about 25 miles south of SFO — roughly a 30–40 minute drive down Hwy 101, depending on traffic.
How do I get from SFO to the RV?
Most fly-in guests take a short rideshare from the terminals to our Mountain View base for free self-pickup. We can also coordinate delivery to align with your arrival — just share your flight details.
Can you time pickup to my flight?
Yes. Send us your inbound flight information and we'll schedule pickup or delivery so your fully stocked motorhome is ready close to when you land.
What's included for travelers flying in?
Every RV comes with 100+ items — bedding, full kitchen gear, camp chairs, and sewer kit — so you don't need to fly with or buy camp supplies. Just pick up groceries near base.
Where can I go from SFO on an RV trip?
From our base you're well placed for Yosemite (~4 hrs), Big Sur and Hwy 1 (2.5 hrs), Lake Tahoe (~4 hrs), Napa and Sonoma (2 hrs), and the Redwoods to the north.
