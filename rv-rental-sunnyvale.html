FAQ

Common Questions

How far is Crystal RV from Sunnyvale?
We're about 5 miles away at 1001 La Avenida St, Mountain View — roughly a ten-minute drive from downtown Sunnyvale via El Camino Real or Central Expressway.
Do you deliver RVs to Sunnyvale?
Yes. We deliver throughout the South Bay — Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, Cupertino, and beyond. Rentals of 5+ nights receive complimentary delivery within our primary service zone.
Can I self-pickup the RV?
Yes — self-pickup is always free at our Mountain View base, daily 8AM–6PM by appointment, with a full 30–60 minute orientation. Sunnyvale guests usually arrive in about ten minutes.
Is Crystal RV good for Levi's Stadium events?
Very — Levi's Stadium is about ten minutes north. Many guests rent an RV for concert and game weekends to skip surge hotel pricing and keep their group together.
What's the minimum rental period?
The minimum rental is 3 nights for all vehicles, with no maximum. Many Sunnyvale guests take longer 1–3 week California road trips.
