Trip Ideas

Best Family RV Road Trips
from the Bay Area

All within striking distance from the Bay. Our team has personally done every one of these with kids in tow.

🏔️
3.5 hrs from Bay Area

Yosemite National Park

The crown jewel of California family road trips. Valley View, Bridalveil Fall, and the Mirror Lake loop are perfect for all ages. Book campsite well in advance for summer visits.

🌊
2–3 hrs south

Big Sur & Highway 1

The most iconic coastal drive in California. Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park has family-friendly trails and an oceanside campground. Kids love the McWay Falls overlook.

⛄
3.5–4 hrs northeast

Lake Tahoe

Crystal-clear water, sandy beaches, and mountain air. D.L. Bliss State Park has some of the best family-friendly camping right on the lake. Summer is magical.

🌲
5–6 hrs north

Redwood National Park

Walk among the world's tallest trees. The Avenue of the Giants and Stout Grove are family favorites. Kids are awestruck every time — guaranteed.

🌺
1.5–2 hrs south

Pinnacles National Park

California's most underrated park. Talus cave exploration, condor sightings, and spring wildflowers. The Balconies Cave trail is an adventure kids won't forget.

🏜️
5 hrs east

Death Valley (Fall–Spring)

Otherworldly landscapes that feel like another planet. Visit October–March for comfortable temperatures. Badwater Basin and Zabriskie Point are the must-sees.

FAQ

Family RV Rental California — Questions

How many people can sleep in your RVs?
All four of our Class C motorhomes sleep up to 6 people. Most feature a queen master bed for parents, a cab-over bunk for kids (rated to 350 lbs), and a convertible dinette bed. The exact configuration varies by vehicle — view individual listings for details. We'll recommend the best layout for your family size during booking.
Is there a minimum age for children in the RV?
There's no minimum age for passengers — infants and toddlers are welcome. All standard California child car seat laws apply while driving. We don't supply car seats, so please bring your own if needed. The RV has standard seat belts in the cab area; passengers should be seated and belted when the vehicle is in motion.
What camping gear is included for families?
Every rental includes: complete kitchen cookware, dishes, silverware, K-cup coffee maker, kettle, toaster, microwave, board games, up to 6 camp chairs, fresh bed sheets, RV toilet paper, first aid kit, and all hookup equipment. Pillows, blankets, and towels are available as a $15/set add-on. You just need groceries, personal items, and your sense of adventure.
Is the RV safe for families with young children?
Yes — every Crystal RV goes through a 100-point safety inspection before every rental. The cab-over bunk includes a safety net or mattress rail. We go through all safety features during the orientation, including the smoke detector, CO detector, fire extinguisher, and propane shut-off. We're a family ourselves — safety is never an afterthought.
What's the best California campground for families with kids?
Our top picks for Bay Area families: (1) Yosemite Valley — kids are awestruck by the waterfalls and cliffs; (2) Lake Tahoe at D.L. Bliss — clean sandy beaches right on the lake; (3) Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park — redwoods and ocean trails; (4) Samuel P. Taylor near Point Reyes — easy 45-min drive with great forest trails. We provide a full trip planning guide with every booking.
Can we bring our dog on the family trip?
Yes! The 2021 Thor Daybreak 22GOC is our pet-friendly vehicle. A $100 pet fee applies per trip and the security deposit is $1,500. Pets must be potty trained and may not be left unattended in the RV. Dogs, cats, and birds are all welcome. Many California state parks and campgrounds have pet-friendly trails — we'll highlight those in your trip guide.
