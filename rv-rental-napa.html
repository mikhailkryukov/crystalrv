FAQ

Common Questions

How far is Napa from Crystal RV?
Napa is about 75 miles — roughly 90 minutes — from our Mountain View base, via US-101 and CA-37 or up I-880 and I-80.
Which Napa-area campgrounds take RVs?
Skyline Wilderness Park in Napa, Bothe-Napa Valley State Park near Calistoga, the Napa Valley Expo, and several Calistoga RV resorts accept RVs. Book ahead, especially during harvest season.
Can I use the RV for wine tasting?
Yes — that's one of the best reasons to rent one. Bring a designated driver, tour the wineries by day, and return to a comfortable motorhome at night. Always taste responsibly and never drive after drinking.
Do I need reservations for wineries and campgrounds?
Often, yes. Many Napa wineries require tasting appointments, and campgrounds fill up in peak season. Reserve both in advance, and we can point you to the right campground booking sites.
What's the minimum rental period?
The minimum rental is 3 nights for all vehicles, with no maximum. A long weekend is a popular length for a Napa and Sonoma wine-country trip.
