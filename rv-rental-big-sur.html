FAQ

Common Questions

How far is Big Sur from Crystal RV?
Big Sur is about 140 miles — roughly 2.5 hours — from our Mountain View base, via US-101 to Highway 1 through Monterey and Carmel.
Which Big Sur campgrounds take RVs?
Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park, Big Sur Campground & Cabins, Riverside, Limekiln State Park, and Plaskett Creek accept RVs within posted length limits. Many have no hookups, and all book up early.
Do I need reservations?
Yes — Big Sur's campgrounds are small and popular. Reserve well in advance through ReserveCalifornia, Recreation.gov, or the campground operator. We can point you to the right site for each.
Can the RV handle Highway 1?
Yes. Our Class C motorhomes drive Highway 1 comfortably at a relaxed pace. We'll review the route, turnouts, and any seasonal considerations during your orientation.
What about Highway 1 closures?
Highway 1 is fully open through Big Sur again: Caltrans reopened the road at Regent's Slide on January 14, 2026, restoring the full Carmel-to-Cambria route. Winter storms can still cause temporary closures, so check Caltrans QuickMap before you travel and we'll help you plan the approach.
