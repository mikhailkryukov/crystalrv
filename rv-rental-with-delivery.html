FAQ

Common Questions

How much does delivery cost?
Rentals of 5+ nights receive complimentary delivery within our primary Bay Area service zone (spanning SF, Oakland, San Jose, Marin, and the Peninsula). Shorter rentals have a delivery fee based on your distance from Mountain View. Contact us for an exact quote to your address.
Do you deliver to SFO airport?
Yes — SFO airport delivery is available for $100 each way (delivery and/or pickup). Ideal for out-of-state or international guests. Provide your flight details and we'll coordinate the timing. We also deliver to SJC airport for the same $100 fee.
What is the delivery window?
We schedule deliveries between 8AM–8PM daily. We'll coordinate a specific 1-2 hour window the day before your rental starts and confirm within 15 minutes of your exact arrival time. We're never more than a few minutes late.
Can you deliver to a campground?
Yes — if your campground is within our delivery zone, we can deliver and pick up there directly. This works especially well if you're starting your trip from a Bay Area campsite. Just provide the campground address when booking.
What if I live outside the primary delivery zone?
We deliver beyond the primary zone to surrounding counties including Santa Cruz, Monterey, Sacramento, Napa, and Sonoma. Delivery fees increase with distance. Contact us with your address and we'll give you an exact quote.
