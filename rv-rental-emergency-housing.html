FAQ

Common Questions

How quickly can you deliver an RV for emergency housing?
We prioritize emergency housing requests. When inventory is available, we can often deliver within 24 hours of a confirmed booking. Call us directly at (669) 245-5636 for the fastest response — do not wait to book online.
Can a Class C motorhome really serve as temporary housing?
Absolutely. Our motorhomes have everything a family needs for weeks: a private bathroom with shower, a full kitchen with stove, fridge, microwave, and coffee maker, queen master bed plus bunkbeds, A/C and furnace for any season, smart TV, and shore power hookup so you're not running on generator.
Where can we park the RV for extended temporary housing?
Options include: your existing driveway (if the home is not at risk), a friend or family member's driveway, a local RV park with full hookups, or a designated emergency shelter site. We can recommend Bay Area RV parks that accommodate extended stays.
What does extended emergency housing cost?
Our standard nightly rates apply, with discounts available for longer-term rentals. If you're in an active emergency situation, please call us — we'll work with you on rates. We want to help Bay Area families first.
What happens if my emergency situation is caused by a wildfire or flood?
In the event of a declared state of emergency, wildfire, flood, or severe weather event making your home uninhabitable or travel unsafe, we offer full rebooking credits regardless of notice. Travel credits never expire. Please call us as soon as possible so we can help you plan.
