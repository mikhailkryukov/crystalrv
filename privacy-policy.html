Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 10, 2026  |  Last Updated: April 10, 2026

Crystal RV ("we," "us," or "our") operates crystalrv.com. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a customer — especially if you are a California resident.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details: Name, email address, and phone number when you submit an inquiry, contact form, or newsletter signup.
  • Booking information: Rental dates, pickup/delivery address, RV selection, driver's license details, and trip preferences — collected via our Wheelbase booking platform.
  • Payment information: Processed securely by Wheelbase and our payment processor. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URLs — collected via Google Analytics (GA4, ID: G-T587L369L1) and cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, confirm, and manage your RV rental reservation.
  • To communicate about your booking, delivery schedule, or damage/security deposit.
  • To respond to inquiries submitted via our contact form or by phone/email.
  • To send the Crystal RV newsletter (only if you opted in — unsubscribe anytime).
  • To improve our website and booking experience using analytics data.
  • To comply with legal and insurance obligations.

3. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies for analytics (Google Analytics) and to remember your session preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect booking functionality.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

  • Wheelbase — reservation management and payment processing (dealer ID 1440877)
  • Google Analytics — site traffic analytics
  • Mobilitas — liability insurance underwriter
  • Lloyd's of London — collision and comprehensive insurance underwriter

These partners have their own privacy policies and are contractually required to handle your data securely. We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

5. Data Retention

We retain booking and contact records for up to 3 years to comply with insurance and legal requirements. Analytics data is retained per Google's standard GA4 retention settings (14 months by default).

6. Your Rights — California Residents (CCPA)

As a California-based business, we comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we have collected about you.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Opt out of the sale of personal data — we do not sell your data.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

To submit a request, email info@crystalrv.com with the subject line "Privacy Request."

7. Security

We use HTTPS throughout our site and rely on PCI-compliant payment processors. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure, but we apply industry-standard safeguards to protect your information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a child has submitted information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be noted by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
