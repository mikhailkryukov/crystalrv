The North Coast by Motorhome

Take Highway 128 Through Anderson Valley

The easiest RV route to Mendocino skips the tightest coastal curves. Head north on US-101 over the Golden Gate to Cloverdale, then follow CA-128 west through Anderson Valley's vineyards and Hendy Woods redwoods. You'll meet the ocean at the Navarro River, then turn north on Highway 1 to Mendocino village and Fort Bragg.

MacKerricher State Park, three miles north of Fort Bragg, is the classic RV base. It has more than 140 sites across three loops, accepts RVs up to 35 feet, and has a dump station. There are no hookups, which is easy in a self-contained Class C. Smaller parks like Van Damme and Russian Gulch have shorter sites, so check the site length on ReserveCalifornia before you book.

Spend your days on the Mendocino Headlands, at Glass Beach and the Skunk Train in Fort Bragg, at Point Cabrillo Light Station, and tasting in Boonville and Philo on the drive home.

FAQ

Mendocino RV Questions

How long is the drive from the Bay Area to Mendocino in an RV?
Plan on 3.5 to 4 hours and about 190 miles from our Mountain View base via US-101 and CA-128, plus time for traffic over the Golden Gate and a stop in Anderson Valley.
Is Highway 1 to Mendocino OK for a Class C motorhome?
Yes, but the CA-128 route through Anderson Valley is the easier way in. North of Jenner, Highway 1 is slow and very curvy. Our 24 to 25 ft RVs handle it, but use turnouts and allow extra time.
Where can we camp with an RV near Mendocino?
MacKerricher State Park near Fort Bragg takes RVs up to 35 feet and has a dump station. Smaller parks like Van Damme and Russian Gulch have shorter sites, and there are private RV parks in Fort Bragg and Caspar. Book state parks on ReserveCalifornia.
Do the campgrounds have hookups?
MacKerricher's sites have no hookups, which is fine for a self-contained Class C. Our RVs carry fresh water, house batteries, and a generator. For full hookups, look at the private RV parks around Fort Bragg.
When is the best time to RV the Mendocino coast?
September and October often bring the clearest weather. Winter and early spring are quieter, with whale watching but more rain. Summer is busiest, so reserve early.
