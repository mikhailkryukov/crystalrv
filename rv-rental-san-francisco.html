FAQ

San Francisco RV Rental Questions

Do you deliver RVs to San Francisco neighborhoods?
Yes — we deliver to all SF neighborhoods including the Mission, Sunset, Richmond, Pacific Heights, Noe Valley, SOMA, and more. Rentals of 5+ nights receive complimentary delivery within our primary zone. For shorter rentals, a delivery fee applies based on distance from Mountain View.
Can I get an RV delivered to SFO airport?
Yes! We offer SFO airport delivery for $100. Ideal for out-of-state or international guests who want to start their California road trip right at the airport. Contact us with your flight details and we'll coordinate the timing.
Where do I park an RV overnight in San Francisco?
San Francisco city streets have strict RV parking rules, so most guests use our delivery as a "pickup" — we deliver to your SF address, you load up and drive that day to a campground. Top nearby options: Kirby Cove in the Marin Headlands (1 hour), Samuel P. Taylor State Park (45 min north), or Bodega Dunes (90 min north).
How long is the drive from Mountain View to San Francisco?
About 35–50 minutes depending on traffic. We factor Bay Area traffic into your delivery window and confirm timing with you the day before. Peak hours (7–9AM and 4–7PM) may add time — we'll schedule accordingly.
What's the best RV road trip from San Francisco?
Our most popular SF trip is the Coastal Highway 1 route south through Half Moon Bay, Santa Cruz, and down to Big Sur — gorgeous any time of year. Heading north, Bodega Bay, Point Reyes, and the Sonoma Coast are stunning 1–2 hour drives. For a classic adventure, Yosemite is 3.5 hours east.
