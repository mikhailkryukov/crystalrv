FAQ

Common Questions

How many people can you accommodate in a group rental?
Each of our 4 Class C motorhomes sleeps up to 6, so we can accommodate groups of up to 24 across all 4 vehicles. For larger groups, contact us — we may be able to source additional vehicles through our network.
Can you accommodate a team of 10–15 people?
Yes — 2–3 vehicles covers a group of 10–18. We deliver all vehicles to the same Bay Area location simultaneously, orient all drivers and crews, and you depart together as a convoy.
Do you offer corporate invoicing?
Yes — we can work with company billing departments for invoice payment. Contact us before booking to discuss payment terms, certificates of insurance, and any custom requirements for your corporate travel policy.
What makes an RV off-site better than a traditional retreat?
Traditional resorts feel like an extended conference. An RV trip forces your team to work together in a new environment — navigating, cooking, setting up camp — which builds genuine bonds. Teams consistently rate RV off-sites as their most memorable company experience.
Can we add team activities to our rental?
Absolutely. We can recommend local outfitters for group activities near your campground: guided hikes, kayaking, mountain biking, rock climbing, and more. We'll include recommendations tailored to your destination when you book.
