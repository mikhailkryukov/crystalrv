FAQ

Common Questions

How close is Crystal RV to Santa Clara and Sunnyvale?
We're at 1001 La Avenida St in Mountain View — typically 5–10 minutes from most of Santa Clara and Sunnyvale depending on traffic. Free self-pickup makes us the most convenient RV rental in Silicon Valley.
Do you deliver to Santa Clara and Sunnyvale?
Yes — delivery to both cities is available. Rentals of 5+ nights receive complimentary delivery within our primary service zone (which includes Santa Clara and Sunnyvale). Shorter rentals have a small distance-based fee.
Are you the closest RV rental to Levi's Stadium?
Yes — Crystal RV in Mountain View is the closest premium RV rental to Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara. We're about 8 minutes away. That makes us the go-to choice for 49ers games, stadium concerts and other big events.
What's the best campground near Santa Clara?
For local car camping: Coyote Valley RV Resort (20 mi south, full hookups), Uvas Pines RV Park (25 mi southwest), or Henry W. Coe State Park (35 mi east). For a full road trip: Yosemite, Big Sur, or Lake Tahoe are all within 2–4 hours.
Can I book an RV for a corporate team outing?
Yes! We work with tech companies and startups for team off-sites, company retreats, and group trips. Multiple vehicles can be booked together. Contact us directly for group rates and custom arrangements.
