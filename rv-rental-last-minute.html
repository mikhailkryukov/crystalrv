FAQ

Common Questions

What is the shortest notice you accept for RV bookings?
We can sometimes accommodate bookings with as little as 24 hours notice, subject to availability and prep time. For same-day delivery, call us directly at (669) 245-5636 — we'll tell you immediately whether it's possible. Checking online availability first is the fastest way to see what's open.
Is there a premium charge for last-minute bookings?
No — our rates are the same regardless of booking lead time. However, last-minute availability is limited, especially on weekends and during peak season (May–September). Booking even a week ahead significantly improves your options.
What if the RV I want isn't available?
If your preferred vehicle isn't available, we'll recommend an available alternative from our 4-vehicle fleet. All our motorhomes are similar in size and amenities — we'll find the best fit for your crew.
How long does it take to prepare the RV after booking?
We typically need at least 4–6 hours to fully prep, stock, and inspect an RV after a confirmed booking. For same-day delivery, morning bookings are most likely to result in afternoon delivery. Call us for specific timing.
What are the best last-minute RV destinations from the Bay Area?
Quick getaways that require minimal planning: Bodega Bay (2 hrs — oceanside camping, reserve at ReserveCalifornia.com), Big Basin Redwoods (1 hr — some walk-in sites available), Pinnacles National Park (2 hrs — less crowded than Yosemite). For Yosemite, even last-minute permits sometimes open up on recreation.gov the morning before.
