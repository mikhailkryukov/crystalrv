The Giant Sequoias in a Class C: Go In Through the North

Sequoia's Generals Highway climbs about 5,000 feet in a few miles of hairpins. The National Park Service advises against vehicles longer than 22 feet between Potwisha Campground and the Giant Forest Museum, and longer than 24 feet between the Foothills Visitor Center and Potwisha. That's the Highway 198 approach from Three Rivers.

Every Crystal RV motorhome is a 24–25 ft Class C, so we don't send guests up that stretch. Instead, take Highway 180 east from Fresno to the Big Stump entrance in Kings Canyon, see the General Grant Tree in Grant Grove, then drive the Generals Highway south to Lodgepole and the General Sherman Tree. NPS recommends this northern route for longer vehicles.

A few side roads stay off-limits whichever way you come: Moro Rock/Crescent Meadow Road and Crystal Cave Road prohibit vehicles over 22 feet, and RVs aren't permitted in the Mineral King campgrounds. In summer, park at the Giant Forest Museum and ride the park shuttle to Moro Rock.

FAQ

Sequoia RV Questions

Can I drive a Crystal RV to the General Sherman Tree?
Yes, as long as you take the northern route. Enter Kings Canyon at the Big Stump entrance on Highway 180, then drive the Generals Highway south to Lodgepole and Giant Forest. Avoid the Highway 198 approach from Three Rivers: NPS advises against vehicles over 22 feet between Potwisha and the Giant Forest Museum, and our RVs are 24 to 25 feet.
Which park entrance should we use?
The Big Stump entrance on Highway 180 east of Fresno. It's the route the National Park Service recommends for longer vehicles, and it puts the General Grant Tree first on your itinerary.
Where can we camp with the RV?
Lodgepole Campground near the Giant Forest and the Grant Grove campgrounds are good fits for a Class C. Dorst Creek Campground is closed for 2026 for disaster recovery. Reserve on Recreation.gov and filter by your RV's length.
Is there gas inside Sequoia or Kings Canyon?
No. NPS notes there is no gasoline in the national parks, so fill up in Fresno or along Highway 180 before you enter, and plan your loop so you exit with fuel to spare.
When is the best time for a Sequoia RV trip?
Late spring through fall is easiest. In winter the Generals Highway between the parks can close after storms and chain controls are common, so check NPS road conditions before you go. We'll review the forecast with you at pickup.
