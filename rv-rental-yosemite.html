FAQ

Common Questions

How far is Yosemite from Crystal RV?
Yosemite Valley is about 195 miles — roughly 3.5 to 4 hours — from our Mountain View base, via I-580 to CA-120 (Big Oak Flat) or CA-140 through Mariposa.
Which Yosemite campgrounds take RVs?
Inside the park, Upper Pines in the Valley plus Hodgdon Meadow, Crane Flat, and Wawona accept RVs within posted length limits. Just outside, Yosemite Pines and Indian Flat offer full hookups. All book up early.
Do I need a reservation to enter Yosemite?
Yosemite's entry and peak-hours reservation rules vary by season and year. Check the National Park Service site for the current requirements before you travel, and reserve campgrounds through Recreation.gov well in advance.
Will the RV handle the drive into the park?
Yes. Our Class C motorhomes handle CA-120 and CA-140 comfortably. We'll cover the route, grades, and any seasonal road or chain notes during your orientation.
What should I know about food storage in Yosemite?
Yosemite is bear country, so food must be stored in the provided bear lockers at campgrounds, not left in the open. We include storage tips and the RV has secure interior storage for travel.
